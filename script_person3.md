## Slide 8 — The hardest cases look human

The two missed zombies had temperatures like humans.

That made them hard to find.

Bite marks can help. But some humans also have bite marks.

We need to count both kinds of mistake.

## Slide 9 — Gate policy and real-world parallels

We would choose the tree that checks temperature and bite marks, for now.

But we need more tests before we trust it.

Stopping a human by mistake also causes harm.

This problem happens in real life too.

A health test may miss a sick person. A bank may block a real payment.

Different mistakes have different costs.
